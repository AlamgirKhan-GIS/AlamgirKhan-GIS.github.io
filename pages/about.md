## 👤 Biography

I am a geospatial researcher from Peshawar, Pakistan, currently pursuing an MSc in Geospatial Information Science and Technology (Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing) at **LIESMARS, Wuhan University**, under the supervision of **Prof. Timo Balz**, supported by the **CSC Chinese Government Scholarship**.

My research sits at the intersection of **synthetic aperture radar, machine learning and humanitarian applications**. For my MSc thesis, I am building a multi-event Sentinel-1 interferometric coherence change detection framework that tracks conflict-induced urban disturbance in the Gaza Strip from 2023 to 2026, from SNAP GPT coregistration of a long coherence time series to change-point segmentation and machine-learning damage prediction. Alongside this, I use SAR offset tracking to study the surge-type Shisper Glacier in the Karakoram.

My background spans geography, geospatial sciences and climate change, from a BS in Geography at Islamia College Peshawar to a Diploma in Climate Change. Looking ahead, I aim to pursue doctoral research in **InSAR, GeoAI and Earth observation foundation models** for humanitarian mapping, water resources and urbanization.
