## 📌 Posters

### Coherence Change Detection with Sentinel-1 SAR for Conflict Damage Monitoring in the Gaza Strip (2023–2026)

**Mohammad Alamgir Khan** · Supervisor: Prof. Timo Balz
**IGWG 2026**, Wuhan University, China · 16–17 October 2026

`Sentinel-1` `InSAR Coherence` `Change Detection` `Humanitarian Mapping` `UNOSAT Validation`

:::{tip} Key message
Sentinel-1 coherence change detection flags conflict damage across the whole Gaza Strip with **79.5% precision** against UNOSAT, making it a high-confidence screening tool for humanitarian damage assessment.
:::

**Highlights**

- A dense stack of **91 Sentinel-1 IW SLC images** (April 2023 – April 2026) covering the whole Gaza Strip
- A combined **Damage Confidence** metric (POST_STD + Mean_diff) that captures both progressive and one-shot destruction
- **4,642 street blocks** (37.8%) flagged with high damage confidence
- Validated against **198,308 UNOSAT damage points**: 79.5% precision, 41.4% recall
