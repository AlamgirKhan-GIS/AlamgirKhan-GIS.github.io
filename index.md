## 👋 About Me

I am an MSc researcher in **Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing** at **LIESMARS, Wuhan University**, supervised by **Prof. Timo Balz**, and a **CSC Chinese Government Scholarship** holder. My research uses **Sentinel-1 SAR interferometry** and **machine learning** to map and understand change on the Earth's surface, from conflict-damaged cities to surging glaciers in the Karakoram.

I am interested in how satellite radar can support **humanitarian response, disaster monitoring and climate adaptation**, especially in data-scarce regions.
